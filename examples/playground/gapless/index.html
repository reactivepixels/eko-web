Press Play A and Play B. Listen to the moment one song becomes the next.

The test

Each round is one continuous piece of music cut at a single sample into two files. One key plays them with two plain <audio> tags, the second fully loaded and started the instant the first ends. The other plays them through EKO. Which is which changes every round.

The reveal

Both players are recorded sample by sample as they play. After you guess, the screen shows what each one actually sent to your speakers, and measures the silence at the seam. Nothing on it is drawn for effect.

Your own album

Drop two or more consecutive tracks from a gapless album (a live record, a DJ mix, a concept album side), or . Each round becomes a real track change from your music.
